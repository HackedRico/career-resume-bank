# Packmule, cheapest-carrier CLI

## Facts
- Won the Grand Prize at HackExample 2024, built by a team of 3 in 24 hours.
- A Go CLI that reads 5 public carrier rate tables and picks the cheapest carrier for a shipment.
- Jordan wrote the rate parser and the comparison logic. The parser reads 2,300 rate rows in under a second.

## Bullets
- [backend] Built a Go CLI in 24 hours on a team of 3 that picks the cheapest carrier from 5 public rate tables.
