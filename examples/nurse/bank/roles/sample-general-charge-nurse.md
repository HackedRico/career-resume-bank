# Sample General Hospital, step-down unit

## Facts
- Morgan leads each shift as charge nurse on a 24-bed step-down unit with telemetry monitoring on every bed: 8 nurses and 3 aides.
- Patient falls on the unit fell from 14 a year to 6 after Morgan started hourly rounding in 2023. The unit's quality council credits the rounding for the drop.
- Morgan has precepted 9 new graduate nurses, and 8 of them were still on the unit a year later.
- Morgan chairs the unit's medication safety committee of 6. Its double-check rule for insulin and heparin cut reported medication events from 22 a year to 9.
- Morgan rebuilt the shift handoff around a one-page SBAR sheet. Handoff fell from 45 minutes to 25, and night shift overtime fell by 30 hours a month.
- Morgan writes the 6-week staffing schedule for 34 staff with the unit manager, and covered the manager's duties for 5 weeks in 2025.
- Morgan led the unit's move to bedside barcode medication administration in Epic: 34 staff trained in 3 weeks, and scan compliance reached 97%. The informatics team led the move for the hospital.
- Morgan runs a 10-minute safety huddle at the start of each shift. Staff raised 46 near misses through it in its first year, and 12 led to a change in practice.
- Morgan mentors 2 charge nurses in training, and wrote the unit's 12-page charge nurse orientation guide.
- Morgan sits on the hospital's rapid response review group, which reviewed 60 calls in 2025. The unit's rapid response calls fell from 31 to 22 that year.
- Morgan sat on 14 hiring panels in 2 years with the unit manager, and the unit hired 11 nurses from them.
- Morgan built a float list of 12 part-time staff, and the unit's use of agency nurses fell from 9 shifts a month to 2.
- Morgan audits 10 charts a week for fall and restraint notes. Documentation compliance rose from 82% to 96%.
- The unit's patient experience score for nurse communication rose from the 61st percentile to the 78th over 2 years, on the hospital's survey.
