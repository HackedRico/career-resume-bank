# Sample General Hospital, medical-surgical unit

## Facts
- Morgan cared for 5 to 6 patients a shift on a 32-bed medical-surgical unit.
- Morgan started a discharge teaching checklist for heart failure patients. Their 30-day readmissions from the unit fell from 19% to 13% in a year. The cardiology nurse educator reviewed the checklist.
- Morgan joined the unit's pressure injury prevention team, which audits skin on all 32 beds each week. Hospital-acquired pressure injuries fell from 11 a year to 4.
- Morgan was one of 4 super users for the Epic barcode medication workflow and trained 40 staff on it.
- Morgan floated to the COVID unit for 5 months in 2020, caring for 4 patients a shift on high-flow oxygen.
- Morgan took 18 student nurses through clinical rotations on the unit over 2 years.
- Morgan wrote a one-page heparin drip quick guide that 3 units adopted.
- Morgan was the unit's relief charge nurse for 14 months before moving to the step-down unit.
- Morgan won the DAISY Award in 2021, on a nomination from a patient's family.
