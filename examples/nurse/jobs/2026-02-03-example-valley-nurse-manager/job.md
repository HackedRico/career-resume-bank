## Post

Clinical Nurse Manager, Progressive Care. Example Valley Health, Cincinnati, OH.

About the role

Leads our 30-bed progressive care unit: its staffing, its quality measures and the growth of 45 nurses and aides.

What you will do

- Own unit quality measures: patient falls, pressure injuries, medication events and patient experience.
- Hire, schedule, coach and evaluate staff.
- Manage the unit budget with the director of nursing.
- Lead the adoption of new clinical workflows in Epic.

Minimum qualifications

- Bachelor of Science in Nursing.
- Current RN license in Ohio.
- 5 years of acute care nursing experience.
- 2 years of experience as a charge nurse or in another leadership role.
- BLS and ACLS.

Preferred qualifications

- Master's degree in nursing or healthcare administration.
- PCCN or CCRN certification.
- Experience managing a budget.
- Experience with Epic.
- Lean or Six Sigma training.
